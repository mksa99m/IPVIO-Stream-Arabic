# IPVIO Stream 1.1

Free, open-source (GPL-3.0) IPTV player for Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV. Xtream, M3U and Stalker. No ads, no tracking, no account.

## New in 1.1
- **In-app updates** from GitHub Releases (bell icon, one click)
- **Second stream** in the live player: picture-in-picture or split screen, sound from either stream, swap streams. Size, corner and layout are saved
- **Sports hub** with filters by sport and time (now / today / tomorrow / 48 h)
- **Several playlists at once**, merged movies/series, and a playlist switcher right in the Live TV list
- **Rewind and pause live TV**, record the current show (internal or USB)
- **Stalker/Ministra** portals (live + movies), M3U from file, DASH (.mpd)
- **Settings > View** rebuilt with live previews of every option
- **Dutch** added: now 10 languages
- Much faster loading of big Xtream accounts, Xtream fallback via player_api.php
- Wi-Fi transfer of favorites/history/settings, QR codes, optional Google Drive backup
- Crash fixes (duplicate list keys in sports / live TV)
